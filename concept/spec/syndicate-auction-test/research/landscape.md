# Landscape — occupancy at three levels

**Read this first.** The idea as brought was occupied in full by a game one month old.

## Level 1 — the theme: bid on unopened storage units, sell what's inside

**Occupied twice, at ~245M combined visits, and one occupant is a breakout in progress.**

| game | visits | likes | peak CCU | age |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bid Battles! | **193,698,342** | 90.1% | 17,755 (Jul 2022) | ~4 years [research: https://www.rolimons.com/game/9603033881] |
| Storage Hunters: Open World | **50,915,641** | 94.6% | **47,940** | **~1 month** [research: https://www.rolimons.com/game/98800969324557] |

Bid Battles' store copy is the idea verbatim: *"Storage wars! Win garage auctions and find
the rarest treasures in the game. Sell your treasures to customers in your store and grow
your empire!"* [research: https://www.rolimons.com/game/9603033881]

Storage Hunters: *"Bid on auctions, grab your loot, load up your vehicle, and bring
everything back to your shop to sell for profit."* It was **31,658 concurrent at time of
fetch**, average playtime 27.9 minutes, one month after launch.
[research: https://www.rolimons.com/game/98800969324557]

**The newer one is not a stale incumbent, it is actively winning.** A peak CCU of 47,940 in
the first month puts it above Dragon Adventures' all-time peak.

## Level 2 — the mechanism: competitive bidding under partial information

**Occupied precisely, including the details that felt like the design.** Sourced from a guide
site after two other source kinds failed (see below):

- **Real-time bidding against other real players.** *"An auction timer starts"* and *"other
  players bid against you in real time"*; *"in multiplayer, you compete with other players at
  the same auction, which drives bid prices up."*
- **Partial information before bidding, by design.** *"You get a partial view through the
  door gap when the auction opens."* Explicitly the only information source; no full preview.
- **Losing money is possible.** *"Overpay and your profit margin shrinks."*
- **Capacity limits on hauling.** *"Cargo capacity matters more than speed at mid-game"*, with
  vehicle upgrades increasing it, *"preventing unlimited hoarding."*

[research: https://gamelandinsider.com/storage-hunters-open-world-guide]

Every mechanic the idea implied is already shipping. **The peek-through-the-gap, the live PvP
bid, and the load limit are genre-standard, not distinctions.**

## Level 3 — the recurring terms

- **`auction`, `bid`, `lot`, `unit`, `locker`, `storage`** — all occupied by both games.
  Usable, but they carry no signal and they place the game inside the occupied family.
- **`syndicate`, `split`, `share`, `stake`** — no Roblox game surfaced using any of these as a
  signature term. **Free in this combination**, and see below for why that matters.
- **`treasure`, `loot`** — occupied broadly across the platform; generic.

## What searching outside the family found

The adjacent families are all occupied too, which is worth knowing because it closes the
obvious escape routes:

- **Thrift / resale:** Hit The Thrift (hunt racks, clean at the laundromat, resell), Pawn Shop
  Simulator, and Thrift Score (a Salvation Army brand game).
  [research: https://www.roblox.com/games/122454884469606/Hit-The-Thrift]
- **Blind-box unboxing:** Unboxing Simulator, UnBox BlindBoxes.
- **Competitive bidding as its own genre:** Bid For Brainrot — *"join crazy auction duels, bid
  your cash, and collect the rarest Brainrots"* — is player-versus-player bidding with no
  storage framing at all. [research: https://www.roblox.com/games/73296902324416/Bid-For-Brainrot]

So the family is not one game deep. Bidding, partial-information reveal, resale and
refurbishment are each independently occupied.

## The one thing nothing does

**Every occupant is solo: one player bids, one player profits.**

Across all six games checked, no bid is shared and no proceeds are split. Bid For Brainrot is
explicitly a *duel*. Bid Battles and Storage Hunters both put the winnings in one shop owned
by one player.

And **progression in the leading occupant is one-way**: the guide describes reinvestment
cycles, vehicle upgrades and rising bid limits *"with no mention of permanent setbacks or
resource loss mechanics."* [research: https://gamelandinsider.com/storage-hunters-open-world-guide]
Overpaying shrinks a margin; it does not set you back.

**The design took both gaps:**

1. **Syndicate bidding** — players pool cash to afford a lot none could take alone, on a split
   agreed **before** the contents are revealed.
2. **Real downside** — a syndicate can lose, and reputation as a partner persists.

See `00-CORE.md`.

## What stayed unverified

- **The auction mechanic's exact numbers** (timer length, bid increments, capacity figures).
  Three source kinds tried: the Fandom wiki returned **HTTP 402**, techwiser returned
  **HTTP 307**, and the guide site that succeeded describes mechanics qualitatively without
  figures. No numbers obtained. The settling fetch would be the Fandom `Auctions` page via a
  different route, or in-game observation.
- **Whether any occupant has ever tried a shared-bid feature and removed it.** Absence in six
  store descriptions and one guide is weak evidence. Patch notes or a dev forum thread would
  settle it and neither was found.
- **Bid Battles' current activity.** Its peak CCU is dated July 2022; no current-player figure
  was fetched, so "incumbent" is a scale claim and not an activity claim.
