# Ruin restoration — concept

*Everyone downstream reads this. Working name pending; slug `incremental-spinoff-v2`.*

A relaxing Roblox restoration game where you cut back overgrowth to reclaim a ruin, and
find what was buried under it.

**Fantasy.** An overgrown ruin being reclaimed. You clear vines and moss off ancient stone,
and the clearing *is* the revealing — what you uncover is what you keep.

**Core loop.** Clear an area completely → everything buried in it is revealed and enters a
permanent collection → spend currency on tools that clear faster → move to a deeper,
denser area. **A lap is finishing a space, not hitting a number.**

**Cleared is permanent.** Overgrowth never comes back. That is the whole payoff, and it is
what makes "completely" mean something.

**No rebirth. No offline accumulation.** Both deliberately cut — they served a
number-driven progression this design does not have.

**The hook.** ~24 buried objects in 4 sets of 6, each set tied to area depth, each completed
set granting a permanent bonus. **This is where distinction lives** — nothing in the
`X Incremental` family has a harvest-plus-discovery hybrid.

**Genre.** A **restoration / completion game**, not an incremental — despite being built on
incremental bones. Said plainly because genre-literate players will otherwise arrive
expecting rebirth and idle and not find them.

**Endless, cheaply.** Areas feel endless via shuffled hand-authored chunks with randomised
object placement — not true procedural generation.

**Players.** Shared server, parallel progression, own areas, no interaction.

**Controls.** Movement only. No aiming, no clicking, no abilities. One thumb.

**Audience.** 8–14, casual but genre-literate, ~70% mobile, 10–20 minute sessions.
Motivated by collection, relaxation, completion.

**Objectives.** Session: find one new object. Short-term: complete a set. Long-term: all
four sets. With endless areas, the collection is the only finishable thing.

**Economy.** One currency. Clearing pays out; currency buys clearing speed.

**Monetization.** Permanent multipliers only. **Never content access** — no paid area,
object or set, because a paid-only object turns 100% completion into a purchase.

**Look.** `fantasy-ornate` — ornamented, warm, aged. Relics read as treasure, and green
overgrowth on warm stone keeps rarity tiers legible.

**Accessibility constraint.** Rarity tiers must differ by **shape or silhouette, not only
hue** — colour-only tiers exclude ~1 in 12 boys in this age band from the core signal.

**Onboarding.** Clear → reveal inside the first ten seconds. The first patch you touch has
something under it.

**Hook line.** *Clear the overgrowth, find what's buried.*

**Why it exists.** To prove the `arga` pipeline works end to end. Success is shipped
artifacts, not players.

**Base.** Began as a reskin of `[🌱] Grass Incremental Simulator` (Unequal Games, 38M
visits, 96% likes, peak 10.4K CCU now ~819) and **deliberately diverged.** Three of the
reference's seven systems were cut. Research established the noun cannot differentiate:
grass, lumber, ore, scrap, leaves, snow (×3), slime, souls, sand and pressure-washing are
all occupied, several with identical marketing copy. See `research/landscape.md`.
