# 00 — Core

Framing that every downstream reader needs. Read with `CONCEPT.md`.

## Purpose

**This game exists to prove the `arga` pipeline works end to end.** [you chose: R1 Q3]

The pipeline is the product; the game is evidence it works. Success is **shipped
artifacts, not players** — every creative area produced usable output, the sheets fed them
without gaps, and a playable build with real UI came out the far end.

Target: the **smallest game that still gives every creative area real work.**

**Non-goals:**
- Beating the genre's retention curve. Offered and declined.
- Revenue. Offered and declined — the genre demonstrably earns, and that is not why this
  is being built.
- **Differentiation as an end in itself.** See below: distinction was deliberately located
  in one system rather than in the theme.

## Where distinction comes from

**The hidden-collection layer, not the noun.** [you chose: R1 Q1]

Research established that the noun cannot differentiate: at least two studios ship
`X Incremental` with a shared marketing sentence, and nouns are consumed faster than a new
game can ship — snow alone is taken three times over.
[research: `research/landscape.md`]

So the theme is a **vehicle**, and the distinguishing system is objects revealed by
harvesting that enter a set-structured permanent index. Nothing in the surveyed family has
this. [research: `research/landscape.md`]

**Consequence downstream:** content design is the primary creative work on this project,
not art or marketing.

## Audience

**8–14, mobile-heavy, short sessions.** [you chose: R1 Q4 — asked directly this run; the
previous run inferred this and never asked]

- casual but **genre-literate** — they have played an incremental and expect rebirth to
  behave the way it always does
- ~70% mobile / ~25% desktop / ~5% console [I assumed — the split; the band was chosen]
- 10–20 minute active sessions
- motivated by **collection, relaxation, completion**

Alternatives declined: 13+ incremental enthusiasts wanting nested resets, and 5–10 needing
near-zero text. Recorded because both were live options — this is not a default.

## Genre

Incremental / idle simulator, Roblox sense. [you said]

**But the progression spine changed this run.** See `01-FOUNDATION.md` — a lap is completing
an area, not a rebirth cycle, which makes this less purely prestige-driven than the
reference and more completion-driven.
