## Discovery hook

**"Clear the overgrowth, find what's buried."** [you accepted: R6 Q1]

A dual promise. The reasoning matters:

- **The buried half is the differentiator no competitor can claim.** Every game in the
  `X Incremental` family promises only the harvest. [research: `research/landscape.md`]
- **It gives a thumbnail two composable halves** — a cleared path through green, and a relic
  mid-reveal.
- Pure "restore the ruin" was declined for underselling the collection. "Uncover a lost
  civilisation" was declined because the verb disappears. "Every ruin hides something" was
  declined because it promises secrets the discovery pacing would then have to keep
  delivering.

**Positioning note that matters more than the line:** this is being marketed as a
**restoration game, not an incremental** [you chose: R4 Q2]. That deliberately moves it out
of the category where every competitor sits — at the cost of the incremental audience's
built-in search behaviour.

Left open — the name, icon, thumbnail composition, and store description.

## Live-ops intent

Default in `OPEN.md §2`, overridable. Note that priority 3 already excludes seasons and
events, so the default is constrained by a decision already made.

**One asymmetry worth flagging:** permanent clearing plus endless shuffled areas means new
content can be added as new authored chunks without touching existing systems. This design is
unusually easy to extend, which is a live-ops advantage nobody asked for.
