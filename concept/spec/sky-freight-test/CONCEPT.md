# Sky freight — concept

> **⚠ SYNTHETIC TEST FIXTURE.** No developer was interviewed. Every answer tagged
> `[simulated]` is really an `[I assumed]`, produced to exercise the stage-0 gates. **Do not
> feed this to a build.** Working name pending; slug `sky-freight-test`.

*Everyone downstream reads this.*

A Roblox game where you ride a tamed flying creature as a **freight courier** across an
authored sky archipelago, and the routes you can fly are limited by what your creature can
physically do.

**Fantasy.** A working bush pilot with an animal instead of an aircraft. You are not a
dragon rider and not a hero. You are someone with a good animal and a delivery to make.

**Tone.** Practical, warm, unhurried competence. The job is taken seriously; the world is
gentle. Dry and sparse humor, and only in cargo manifests.

**Core loop.** Take a contract from a depot board → load cargo → fly a route whose terrain
demands a specific capability → deliver → get paid → spend on capability, or tame a creature
with a capability you lack → routes that were closed are now open.

**A lap is one delivery run.** Not a number, not a reset.

**The creature is a key, not a stat.** Four capability classes — glide, climb, haul, and one
held back for later — and the map is authored so a route is impossible without the right
class. This is the distinction, and it is the one thing the landscape survey could not find
anywhere. See `research/landscape.md`.

**The premise it started as is occupied at 1.9 billion visits.** Dragon Adventures (1.67B,
200+ species) and the official How To Train Your Dragon (201M) both ship taming, islands and
flight. Distinction had to move off the fantasy, and did.

**Genre.** A **delivery / logistics game** with a creature-collection surface, not a
creature-collector with deliveries. Said plainly because the audience it needs is the
Trucking Tycoon and Overfreight audience, not the Dragon Adventures audience.

**World.** An authored archipelago, four regions, gated by capability rather than by level.
**Day/night and wind are real:** thermals lift by day, some routes only open in certain wind.

**Players.** Shared server 12–24, parallel contracts, no PvP, no trading. Roblox default
filtered chat is on.

**Controls.** Mobile-first. Movement plus one context button. Flight is hold-to-ascend
against a stamina meter.

**Audience.** 10–16, optimizers rather than collectors, ~65% mobile, 15–25 minute sessions.

**Failure exists.** Cargo can be lost. It costs the contract fee, never progress. There is
friction here on purpose, unlike a cosy game.

**Roster.** ~12 creatures across the four capability classes, structure only.

**Cast.** Yes, minimal: depot handlers as archetypes, no named characters.

**Economy.** One currency. Contracts pay it; capability upgrades and taming costs drain it.

**Monetization.** Cosmetics and convenience only. **Never capability access** — a paid
capability would put map content behind money.

**Look.** `clean-modern`. Warm, sunlit, hand-painted, with a legible logistics interface.

**Onboarding.** One short delivery, completed and paid, inside the first sixty seconds.

**Hook line.** *Every route needs the right animal.*

**Why it exists.** To exercise the `arga` stage-0 gates on a fresh brief. Success is the
gates firing, not players.
