# Research — the creature-taming incumbents

The two games that own the opening premise. Design extracted, not lore.

## Dragon Adventures

[research: https://www.rolimons.com/game/3475397644] ·
[research: https://www.roblox.com/games/3475397644/Dragon-Adventures]

**1,674,243,807 visits · 93.4% likes (1,097,217 up / 77,536 down) · peak CCU 60,951 ·
~7 years old · ~9,145 concurrent at time of fetch.**

Store copy: *"Embark on the adventure of a lifetime in Dragon Adventures, the ultimate pet
dragon taming RPG! Explore breathtaking worlds filled with majestic dragons and hidden
treasures."*

**The loop, as far as sources describe it:** hatch an egg → raise the dragon → explore
biomes → find more species → build a lair. 200+ species. Riding and flight are standard;
flight is entered by double-tapping space.
[research: https://www.gameslearningsociety.org/how-to-fly-in-dragon-adventures-roblox/]

**What retains:** species collection breadth and base building. Currently running a LEGO
NINJAGO crossover, which says something about the retention model: licensed events.

**What players of it will expect from any similar game:** many species, egg hatching,
riding, free flight without permission gates, and a home base to display in.

**What the sources did not cover:** any access rule on a biome, per-species capability
differences beyond stats, session length, or monetization detail. Three sources
(Rolimons, the store page, a guide site) describe biomes and species and never an
access gate.

## HOW TO TRAIN YOUR DRAGON (official)

[research: https://www.rolimons.com/game/75663528075786] ·
[research: https://deltiasgaming.com/how-to-train-your-dragon-dragons-guide-roblox/]

**201,154,518 visits · 94.3% likes (197,579 up / 11,953 down) · peak CCU 33,164 ·
created ~March 2025 · ~3,907 concurrent at time of fetch.**

Store copy: *"Explore the world of How To Train Your Dragon as you travel across the
islands, take on quests, meet legendary characters, and train your dragon to become
stronger through every battle and discovery."*

**This is the dangerous one.** It ships the opening premise almost exactly: islands,
taming, open-world flight, dragon bonding, and per-dragon flight *handling* differences.
Taming is a short minigame rather than a resource cost. Dragons unlock by rescuing them
from islands, on a staggered release schedule.

**Live-ops model:** timed events with exclusive dragon prizes (Trial of Flame, three
arenas), plus race and target-practice modes.

**The one thing it does not do:** capability-class gating. Dragons differ in speed and
manoeuvrability, not in *what kind of movement they make possible*. Islands are cleared,
not unlocked by capability.

**Non-transferable:** it holds a licensed IP. Competing on the fantasy means competing
against name recognition that cannot be bought.

**What the sources did not cover:** monetization, session length, retention numbers, and
whether any island has an access requirement. Two guide sites and one stats aggregator.

## What both incumbents imply for this design

- **Species breadth is their moat and cannot be beaten.** 200 species and a licence.
  Competing on roster size is losing on their terms.
- **Free flight is the genre default.** Any restriction on where you may fly is a
  deliberate divergence and will read as friction to a player arriving from either game.
  That is a real cost of the traversal-gating decision and it must be designed for, not
  assumed away.
- **Both are exploration-and-collection games.** Neither is an economy game. A freight
  loop is the axis they are not competing on.
