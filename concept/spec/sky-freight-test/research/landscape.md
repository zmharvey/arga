# Landscape — occupancy at three levels

**Read this first.** It is the reason the design moved off its opening premise.

Per process step 2, occupancy was checked at all three levels where the idea claimed
distinction, and outside the genre family. Two of the three came back occupied.

## Level 1 — the theme: tame flying creatures across islands

**Occupied, at a scale that ends the discussion.**

| game | visits | likes | peak CCU | age |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dragon Adventures | **1,674,243,807** | 93.4% | 60,951 | ~7 years [research: https://www.rolimons.com/game/3475397644] |
| HOW TO TRAIN YOUR DRAGON (official DreamWorks) | **201,154,518** | 94.3% | 33,164 | ~1 year [research: https://www.rolimons.com/game/75663528075786] |

Dragon Adventures sells itself as *"the ultimate pet dragon taming RPG"* with 200+ dragon
species, egg hatching, riding, flight, and multiple biomes.
[research: https://www.rolimons.com/game/3475397644]

The official HTTYD game is the closer one and the more dangerous. It ships **islands,
taming, open-world flight, and per-dragon flight characteristics** — a Night Fury described
as *"quick, maneuverable, and suitable for beginners"* against other dragons that handle
differently. [research: https://deltiasgaming.com/how-to-train-your-dragon-dragons-guide-roblox/]

**1.9 billion combined visits.** The fantasy is not available, and one occupant holds a
licensed IP this cannot compete with on recognition.

## Level 2 — the mechanism: "each creature changes how you travel"

**Occupied, both outside Roblox and inside it.**

**Palworld** is the canonical implementation. Three riding classes (ground, flying,
swimming); flying mounts ascend on held input at stamina cost; glider Pals give partner
skills worth *"roughly two floors of altitude, ideal for scaling cliffs, reaching high
ledges, or finishing a climb when a flying mount runs out of stamina."*
[research: https://allthings.how/palworld-every-glider-pal-and-what-each-partner-skill-does/]
[research: https://palworld.wiki.gg/wiki/Rideable_Pals]

**Not a Roblox game.** Its monetization (paid up front) and session assumptions (long
desktop sessions) do not transfer, and saying so is part of the finding. What *does*
transfer is player expectation: anyone who has played it will expect creature-as-vehicle to
work at least this well.

**Dig It** (Roblox) has the mechanism natively: mounts *"give you various useful abilities,
such as swimming fast or even flying"*, earned by completing an area's excavation.
[research: https://www.thegamer.com/roblox-dig-it-mounts-unlock-guide/]

So *"each creature changes how you travel"* is not a distinction. It is a genre convention
with a AAA reference implementation.

## Level 3 — the recurring terms

Checked before any sheet committed to a word.

- **`tame` / `taming`** — the family's standard verb. Both level-1 occupants use it in store
  copy. Usable, but it carries no signal.
- **`dragon`** — unusable. Both occupants own it, one by licence.
- **`mount`** — occupied as a *reward-item* noun across several Roblox games (Dig It, World
  Zero, MOUNT RNG). Importing it imports "collectible stat item", not "vehicle".
  [research: https://www.roblox.com/games/133341016381877/MOUNT-RNG]
- **`archipelago`** — no Roblox game surfaced using it as a signature term. Free, but long
  and above the reading level of the stated audience.
- **`freight`, `cargo`, `contract`, `depot`, `route`** — occupied only by vehicle-logistics
  games (below), never by a creature game. **Free in this combination.**

## What searching outside the family found

The family the idea invited comparison to is creature-taming. The nearest neighbour is in a
different genre entirely.

**Delivery Not Guaranteed** — a post-apocalyptic Roblox game where *"civilization uses
blimps and airships as transportation"* and players *"trade packages across the skies"*
between islands, shipping updates that add islands and aircraft.
[research: https://www.roblox.com/games/105574679986189/Delivery-Not-Guaranteed]

This is the **closest existing game to the design that resulted**, and it is not a
creature game at all. Also in the vehicle-logistics space: Shipping Lanes (cargo between
ports), Overfreight (rail freight chains), Trucking Tycoon (40+ trucks, 21 cargo types).
[research: https://www.roblox.com/games/82461912126439/Overfreight]

**No search returned a Roblox game where a tamed creature hauls freight.** The sky-freight
loop exists with vehicles; creature-borne freight did not surface.

## The verdict, and what it changed

Two of the three claimed distinctions were occupied. What remains genuinely open is a pair:

1. **Traversal-type gating** — the map locked behind *which kind* of movement you have, so a
   creature is a **key**, not a stat. Dragon Adventures' 200 dragons overwhelmingly all fly
   and differ by stats and looks; HTTYD's differ by handling, not capability class;
   Palworld's differ by class but its world is not gated on them; Dig It gates the reward on
   the area rather than the area on the reward. Nothing found gates space on capability.
2. **Creature-borne freight** — occupied by airships, not by animals.

**The design took both.** See `00-CORE.md`.

## What stayed unverified

- **Per-delivery pacing in any sky-freight game.** Tried three source kinds on Delivery Not
  Guaranteed: the Roblox page (description only, no numbers), a search for a wiki (none
  found), and a Grokipedia entry (summary only, no timings). No route-duration figure
  obtained. This is the number the whole loop rests on and it remains open.
- **Whether Dragon Adventures has any capability-gated area.** Its own page and two guide
  sites describe biomes and species, never an access rule. Absence of evidence in three
  sources, not evidence of absence.
- **Delivery Not Guaranteed's scale.** No Rolimons fetch attempted for it; visits and CCU
  unknown, so "closest neighbour" is a similarity claim and not a scale claim.
