## Discovery hook

**"Every route needs the right animal."** [simulated: R5 Q6]

The reasoning matters more than the line:

- **It promises the gate, which is the only thing no competitor can claim.** Both incumbents
  promise the animal and the flying; neither promises that the animal is a key.
  [research: `research/landscape.md`]
- **It gives a thumbnail two composable halves** — a creature mid-flight carrying something,
  and a route visibly blocked.
- Pure "tame and ride dragons" was declined: it puts the game directly against 1.9 billion
  visits on their own promise.
- "Deliver cargo across the sky" was declined for hiding the creature entirely, which loses
  the browse-level appeal of an animal in the thumbnail.

**Positioning note that matters more than the line:** this is marketed as a **delivery game
with animals**, not a creature-taming game. [simulated: R1 Q1] That moves it out of the
category both incumbents sit in, at the cost of the creature-collector audience's built-in
search behaviour — a real cost, since that audience is enormous and the logistics audience
on Roblox is smaller.

**Name is not settled.** Left open, with one constraint from research: **`dragon` and `mount`
are both unusable** — the first is owned twice over, the second reads as a collectible stat
item across several Roblox games.
[research: https://www.roblox.com/games/133341016381877/MOUNT-RNG]

## Live-ops intent

Default in `OPEN.md §2`, overridable. Note that priority 3 already excludes seasons, events
and codes, so the default is constrained by a decision already made.

**One asymmetry worth flagging:** an authored archipelago is the *opposite* of cheap to
extend. Every new route is hand-built and must be designed around a capability class. Unlike
a generated or shuffled world, this design cannot add content without authoring effort, which
makes it a poor candidate for a heavy live-ops cadence. Anyone planning updates should know
the content cost per drop is high.

## References and how this differs

| reference | what it shares | how this differs |
|---|---|---|
| Dragon Adventures (1.67B visits) | taming, riding, flight, islands | no species breadth, no base building, freight loop, gated space |
| HOW TO TRAIN YOUR DRAGON (201M) | taming, islands, per-creature flight feel | no licence, no combat, capability classes rather than handling stats |
| Palworld (not Roblox) | creature-as-vehicle with movement classes | its world is not gated on class; also paid-up-front, so its monetization and session assumptions do not transfer |
| Delivery Not Guaranteed | sky freight between islands, package trading | airships rather than animals |
| Trucking Tycoon / Overfreight | contract-and-cargo logistics loop | ground vehicles, no capability gate, no creature |

**The nearest neighbour is not a creature game.** That is the single most useful thing the
survey produced, and it is why the audience decision in `00-CORE.md` moved away from
collectors.
