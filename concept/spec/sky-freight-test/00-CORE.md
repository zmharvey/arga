## Purpose

## Where distinction comes from

**Capability-gated space, plus creature-borne freight.** [simulated: R1 Q1]

The opening premise — tame flying creatures across islands — is occupied at **1.9 billion
combined visits** by two games, one holding a licensed IP.
[research: https://www.rolimons.com/game/3475397644]
[research: https://www.rolimons.com/game/75663528075786]

And the mechanism the idea named as its distinction, *"each creature changes how you
travel"*, is a genre convention with a AAA reference implementation in Palworld and a
shipping Roblox instance in Dig It.
[research: https://palworld.wiki.gg/wiki/Rideable_Pals]
[research: https://www.thegamer.com/roblox-dig-it-mounts-unlock-guide/]

So distinction relocated to the two things the survey could not find:

1. **The map is gated on capability class.** A creature is a key. Nothing found gates space
   on what your animal can do; incumbents gate the reward on the area, never the area on the
   ability. [research: `research/landscape.md`]
2. **Freight carried by an animal.** The sky-freight loop exists on Roblox, with airships.
   [research: https://www.roblox.com/games/105574679986189/Delivery-Not-Guaranteed]
   No search returned a creature doing it.

**Consequence downstream:** route and region authoring is the primary creative work on this
project, not the creature roster. The roster serves the gate.

## Audience

**10–16, optimizers, mobile-heavy, medium sessions.** [simulated: R1 Q4]

- **Deliberately not the incumbents' audience.** Dragon Adventures' draw is species
  collection; this game's draw is route planning. The nearest audience is the
  vehicle-logistics one (Trucking Tycoon, Overfreight, Shipping Lanes).
- ~65% mobile / ~30% desktop / ~5% console [simulated: R1 Q4 — the split is assumed, the
  band was chosen]
- 15–25 minute active sessions, longer than a cosy game because a route has to complete
- motivated by **optimization, route mastery, and completion** — not by collection breadth

Declined: 8–14 collectors (loses to Dragon Adventures on its own terms) and 13+ simulation
enthusiasts wanting deep supply chains (too much system for the scope).

## Genre

**Delivery / logistics, with a creature surface.** [simulated: R1 Q1]

Stated in this order on purpose. A genre-literate player arriving expecting a creature
collector will find a roster of twelve and be disappointed; one arriving for a delivery game
will find the roster is the interesting part of the delivery game.
