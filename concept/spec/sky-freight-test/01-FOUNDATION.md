## Theme / fantasy

**A working freight courier who flies a tamed animal.** [simulated: R2 Q2]

You are a bush pilot whose aircraft is alive. The animal is equipment you have a
relationship with, and the job is ordinary work done well.

What the direction fixes:

- **Not a dragon rider.** No heroism, no destiny, no bond-of-legend. That ground is held by
  a licensed game with 201M visits and cannot be won.
  [research: https://www.rolimons.com/game/75663528075786]
- **The animal is a vehicle with opinions**, not a pet and not a weapon.
- **Cargo is the point.** Everything the player does is in service of moving something from
  one place to another.
- **The world is inhabited and working.** Depots exist, someone sent the cargo, someone is
  waiting for it.

Left open — what the creatures are, what the region and depot names are, who sends the
cargo and why, the whole fiction of the archipelago. *[kind of work: naming and fiction]*

## Tone

**Practical, warm, unhurried competence.** [simulated: R2 Q1]

Asked as its own question rather than inherited from the theme, because theme says what the
fiction is and tone says how it is played. A freight courier can be played as grizzled,
whimsical, or corporate, and those are three different games to name and score.

| dimension | decision |
|---|---|
| register | plain and observational. The game reports; it does not exclaim. |
| **humor level** | **dry and sparse, and only in cargo manifests.** Nowhere else is funny: no system copy, no error, no tutorial, no store copy. No creature name is a pun. |
| seriousness | the job is taken seriously. The world is gentle. |
| emotional beats | two peaks only — a delivery landing, and a new capability opening a route that was closed. Nothing else peaks. |

**Tonal do-nots:** no epic destiny, no peril or dread, no corporate-satire framing, no
loud congratulation. The player is competent and the game does not need to tell them so.

**Why sparse humor and not none:** a 10–16 optimizer audience will read complete
straight-facedness as dry rather than warm, and a cargo manifest is the one surface where a
light line costs nothing because it does not repeat. [simulated: R2 Q1]

## Core loop

**Interviewed as its own round.** [simulated: R1 Q2]

| # | player action | produces |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | take a contract from a depot board | a destination, a cargo type, and a capability requirement |
| 2 | load the cargo onto your creature | weight, which changes how the creature handles |
| 3 | fly the route | terrain and weather that demand the required capability |
| 4 | deliver at the destination depot | currency, scaled by cargo value and condition on arrival |
| 5 | spend on capability, or tame a creature with a class you lack | access to routes that were closed |

**A lap is one delivery run.** [simulated: R1 Q2] Not a threshold and not a reset.

**How it closes:** step 5 changes which contracts on the board in step 1 are takeable. The
board is filtered by what you can physically fly, so new capability visibly opens new work.

**Lap 1 versus lap 100:** early routes are short, single-capability, and calm. Later routes
chain two capability demands and cross weather. The binding constraint moves from *which
class you own* to *route planning under wind and daylight*.
[simulated: R1 Q2]

**Lap length: unknown.** No sky-freight game's per-route duration could be sourced across
three source kinds. See `research/landscape.md`. **This is the number the loop rests on and
it is open.** *[kind of work: all numeric curves and route timings]*

## The divergence from the opening idea, stated plainly

The idea proposed that "each creature changes how you travel" was the distinction. Research
found that is a genre convention with a AAA reference implementation, and that the fantasy
around it is occupied at 1.9 billion visits.

**Two things changed as a result:**

1. **Distinction moved from the capability itself to the gating of space by capability.** The
   creature became a key rather than a stat.
2. **The loop became freight rather than exploration**, because freight is the axis neither
   incumbent competes on.

**Consequence: a real cost was accepted.** Free flight anywhere is the genre default in both
incumbents. Restricting where a player may fly will read as friction to anyone arriving from
them, and that has to be designed for rather than assumed away.
[research: `research/creature-taming-incumbents.md`]
