# Snow-clearing incremental — concept

*Everyone downstream reads this. Working name pending; slug `incremental-spinoff`.*

A relaxing Roblox incremental where you walk through snow to clear it, and find things
buried underneath.

**Fantasy.** A kid with a snow tool that upgrades visibly — shovel to plow to something
absurd — clearing drifts and pushing back a storm. Warm-hearted, low-stress, not grim.

**Core loop.** Move through snow and it clears on contact, paying out by snow tier →
spend on three upgrade axes (value, spawn rate, clear radius) → hit a rebirth threshold →
reset for permanent multipliers → unlock the next, colder zone, which has denser and rarer
snow. Target 45–90 seconds per early lap.

**The hook.** Clearing reveals buried objects that enter a permanent collection: ~24
objects in 4 sets of 6, each set tied to a snow tier, each completed set granting a
permanent bonus. This is the one addition to an otherwise inherited skeleton, and it is
what the reference does not have.

**Genre.** Incremental / idle simulator. Prestige-rebirth spine, zone unlocks, collection
completion.

**Players.** Shared server, 12–20, parallel progression. You see other people; you cannot
interact with them.

**Controls.** Movement only. No aiming, no clicking, no abilities. One thumb.

**Audience.** 8–14, casual but genre-literate, ~70% mobile, 10–20 minute sessions.
Motivated by collection, relaxation and completion.

**Objectives.** Session: find one new object. Short-term: complete a set. Long-term: all
four sets. No mastery layer.

**Economy.** Two currencies — snow currency for upgrades, rebirth currency for permanent
multipliers and zone unlocks.

**Monetization.** Permanent multipliers plus one oversized-tool whale SKU. No cosmetics.
No content gated behind payment.

**Look.** `cartoon-vibrant` — saturated, chunky, thick outlines, loud payoffs. Vibrant
because white snow washes out a pale palette, not because the tone is aggressive.

**Hook line.** *Clear the snow, find what's buried.*

**Scope.** Two zones, three axes, rarity ladder, rebirth, four collection sets. Nothing
else.

**Why it exists.** To prove the `arga` pipeline works end to end. Success is shipped
artifacts, not concurrent players — so this is the smallest game that still gives every
department real work.

**Base.** A deliberate reskin of `[🌱] Grass Incremental Simulator` (Unequal Games, 38M
visits, 96% likes, peak 10.4K CCU now ~819). Skeleton inherited on purpose; fiction and
the buried-object hook are new. Grass, lumber, ore and generic resources are already
taken by shipped games.
