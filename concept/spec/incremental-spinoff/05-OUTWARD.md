## Discovery hook

**"Clear the snow, find what's buried."** [you accepted: R4 Q3]

A dual promise, chosen over three single-promise alternatives. The reasoning matters for
Marketing:

- **The buried half is the actual differentiator.** Lumber Incremental, Ore Incremental
  and Resource Incremental all promise only the harvest. [research] Leading on discovery
  is the one claim none of them can make.
- **It gives a thumbnail two composable halves** — a cleared path, and a revealed object
  mid-reveal.
- Pure before/after was declined because every cleaning game makes that promise. Pure
  power fantasy was declined because it buries the hook. Comedy was declined because the
  game is not designed to deliver on it.

**Caveat worth carrying:** discovery matters less here than it normally would, because
success is defined as shipped artifacts rather than players. [you accepted: R1 Q3]
Marketing should treat this as a real brief, not a growth mandate.

Left open — the name, the icon, the thumbnail composition,
and the store description. *[currently: Discovery & Marketing]*
