# 03 — Meta

**Layer 3 — meta.** Anyone working on objectives, world structure, replayability,
monetization or scope reads this, plus layers 1–2 above it.

## Objectives

| scope | objective | measurable |
|---|---|---|
| moment | clear the patch in front of you | snow disappears on contact |
| **session** | **find at least one new buried object** [you accepted: R4 Q1] | collection count increased this session |
| short-term | complete one set | 6 of 6 objects in a set |
| long-term | complete all four sets | 24 of 24 objects |
| mastery | *none designed* | — |

**The session objective is deliberately tied to the hook, not to a number.** Hitting a
rebirth was the alternative and was declined: it is exactly what the reference does, so
it would add nothing distinguishing. Making discovery the unit of session success is what
makes this game feel unlike Grass Incremental while sharing its skeleton.

That puts a real tuning burden on whoever tunes content — discovery rates must be
generous enough that a typical session yields at least one find, or the stated session
goal silently fails. This is the most likely place for the design to break.

**No mastery objective.** [I assumed — nothing in the scope supports expert play; there
is no execution skill in a proximity-clearing game to master.] Recorded so Meta &
Content does not invent one.

## World

- **Two zones**, gated on rebirth milestones. [you accepted: R2 Q4]
- **Escalating cold** as the progression read — somewhere ordinary, then somewhere
  hostile. Legible without a tutorial. [you accepted: R1 Q2]
- Each zone carries its own stacking multiplier upgrades. [research]
- Denser and rarer snow in the later zone, which is what makes unlocking it feel like a
  reward rather than a change of scenery. [research: the reference's model]

Left open — what the two zones
actually are. Deliberately not settled here: zone count and the gating mechanism are
constraints, zone identity is craft. *[currently: Theme & Narrative + Meta & Content]*

## Replayability

**Banked offline snow plus visible set progress.** [you accepted: R4 Q2]

Both fall out of systems already chosen, so retention costs nothing extra: offline
accumulation makes returning rewarding, and a half-finished set is a visible open loop
the player carries between sessions.

A rotating daily object was declined as out of first-version scope. Doing nothing at all
was offered — reasonable given the purpose — and also declined, so **Meta & Content does
have a retention brief**, just a cheap one.

## Monetization stance

**Match the reference: sell permanent multipliers.** [you accepted: R1 Q4]

The reference sells only 2x multipliers on axes the player is already grinding, plus one
2,500-Robux oversized tool, and zero cosmetics — across 38M visits at a 96% like ratio.
[research: https://www.rolimons.com/game/133086043677134]

- **Allowed:** permanent multipliers on value, luck, rebirth rate, walkspeed; an
  oversized-tool whale SKU. The visible tool ladder chosen in R2 Q3 is the natural home
  for that SKU.
- **Not gating content behind payment.** [I assumed — research surfaced no paid-only zone
  or tier in the reference, so "match it" implies not gating. The `nothing that gates
  content` option was separate and unpicked, so this inference is worth a check.]
- Players of this genre expect to buy speed and do not resent it; the loop *is* numbers
  going up, so a 2x is the product rather than a compromise. [research]

Left open — the actual SKU ladder, price points, and which axis the
whale item sits on. *[currently: Monetization]*

## Scope & priority

**Priority 1 — the first shippable version:**
proximity clearing · three upgrade axes · snow rarity ladder · rebirth · two zones ·
buried-object collection with four sets.

**Priority 2 — after it works:**
additional zones · a duplicate-handling refinement · light social touches.

**Priority 3 — explicitly not in this project:**
codes · daily rewards · leaderboards · trading · pets · automation beyond offline spawn ·
seasons and events.

This ordering is real, not aspirational: the scope answer named the minimum that
exercises every department once, and everything above is what was consciously cut.
[you accepted: R2 Q4]
