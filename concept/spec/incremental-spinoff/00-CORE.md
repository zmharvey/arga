## Purpose

**This game exists to prove the `arga` pipeline works end to end.** [you accepted: R1 Q3]

The real product is the pipeline; the game is the artifact that demonstrates it. That
inverts the usual scope pressure: the target is the **smallest game that still exercises
every creative area and reaches `ui-forge`**, not the best game.

**Success is shipped artifacts, not players.** [you accepted: R1 Q3] Concretely: every
creative area produced usable output, the spec sheets fed them without gaps, and a
playable build with real UI came out the far end.

**Non-goals** — explicitly not being built:
- Beating the reference's retention curve. That was offered and declined; it is where
  scope goes to die. [you accepted: R1 Q3]
- Market differentiation as an end in itself. The buried-object hook exists to give
  departments real work, not to beat Lumber Incremental. [you accepted: step 6 Q4 — the
  R1 tension was put back to you explicitly and resolved this way]

  **Consequence downstream:** marketing and content work should not optimise for a market
  this build is not competing in.

## Genre

Incremental / idle simulator, in the Roblox sense: numbers climb, resets multiply.
[you said]

Progression shape: **prestige-rebirth** as the spine, with **zone unlocks** gated behind
rebirth milestones, and a **collection-completion** layer if the roster hook lands.
[you said + research: https://www.rosenberryrooms.com/grass-incremental/]

## Audience

[you accepted: step 6 Q1 — I inferred all of this from the reference's audience rather
than asking in R1, put it back to you explicitly, and you confirmed it as written.]

- **Age:** 8–14. The genre's core band on Roblox.
- **Experience:** casual, with genre literacy — they have played at least one
  incremental and expect rebirth to work the way it always works.
- **Device mix:** ~70% mobile, ~25% desktop, ~5% console. Proximity-harvest with no
  aiming is why this genre skews so mobile: it plays fine with one thumb.
- **Session:** 10–20 minutes active, plus checking back for offline earnings.
- **Motivations:** `collection`, `relaxation`, `completion`. Low-stress accumulation is
  the appeal; anything that punishes inattention fights the genre.
