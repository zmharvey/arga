## What the brief did not give me

Nine gaps, each routed. None is filled here.

1. **Era, beyond the word *ancient*.** No date, no distance in time, no builder technology level,
   no statement of how long the overgrowth has had. `01-FOUNDATION.md` says *"ancient stone"* and
   `04-PRESENTATION.md` says *"ornamented, warm, aged, crafted"*; nothing else in any sheet touches
   time depth. → sheet `02`. `[cid: decided]` when the sheet lands.
2. **Location, beyond materials.** The brief gives stone, vines, moss and warmth. It never says
   what landscape holds the ruin, what climate it sits in, or whether anything exists outside it.
   → sheet `01`. `[cid: decided]`.
3. **Scale: singular prose against endless design.** *"reclaim a ruin"* (`CONCEPT.md`), *"what the
   ruin actually is"* (`03-META.md`) singular; *"Endless via shuffled authored chunks"* and priority
   2's *"visitable restored **ruins**"* plural. **This is a contradiction in the source, not
   latitude the brief intended.** → sheet `03`. `[cid: decided]`.
4. **The axis of depth is stated twice in two geometries.** *"deeper areas"* (`03-META.md`) reads
   vertical; *"further in means denser overgrowth"* (`01-FOUNDATION.md`) reads horizontal. Nothing
   reconciles them, and Art, layout and the `areas` screen all need one answer. → sheet `03`.
5. **Time of day and weather: total silence.** No sheet in the brief states an hour, a sky, a
   season, a wind, or a rain. Not excluded, not chosen, absent. → sheet `06`. `[cid: decided]`.
   **Carries a second, unroutable question:** whether an ambient light or weather cycle falls under
   the category gate against *"any cyclical, seasonal, renewal ... framing"*. That gate is the
   category lead's inference from priority 3, not a line in the brief, and a diurnal cycle resets
   no progress and regrows nothing. Sheet `06` must state its argument either way. **Flagged upward
   to category verification; not mine to settle.**
6. **Physical law: silence on whether anything here is supernatural.** `02-GAMEPLAY.md` says
   *"**Completing a set grants a permanent bonus**"* and no sheet anywhere explains how a place
   grants anything. The world's law is the precondition for that explanation existing at all.
   → sheet `05`. `[cid: decided]`. **Boundary:** what the bonuses *are* belongs to set-content work
   *[currently: Gameplay — Meta & Content, wave 3]*; sheet `05` decides only whether the world
   permits a supernatural explanation, and names the consequence for them either way.
7. **How a player physically reaches a deeper area.** The brief says no gate is *needed*
   `[I assumed]`; it never says what is there instead, nor whether the place is one continuous
   walkable expanse or a set of discrete arrivals. `04-PRESENTATION.md` lists an `areas` screen at
   priority 2 *"implied by ... moving between areas by depth"*, which presumes a travel or selection
   act no world rule supports. → sheet `07`. `[cid: decided]`. Consequence for arrangement work
   *[Meta & Content, wave 3]* and screen work *[UI/UX, wave 4]*.
8. **What a finished area becomes.** Priority 2 wants restored ruins visitable; the `OPEN.md §2`
   technical default wants finished areas *"collapsed to a completion flag"* to bound save size.
   **Unreconciled in the source.** → sheet `07`, which must decide the world rule without deciding
   the persistence format. `[cid: decided]`.
9. **What exists here at all.** No sheet inventories the world's contents. Fauna, standing water,
   sky, wind, roads, machinery, other people, anything alive: all unmentioned. Silence, not
   exclusion. → sheet `09`. `[cid: decided]`.

## Why 9 sheets

The subject splits along its own seams rather than along a target count. Two sheets exist because
the brief contains a contradiction and an unstated geometry that a single "scale" sheet would blur
(`03` resolves one ruin or many and which way depth points; `04` answers why it never runs out and
why that must not read as generated: these are separate questions and the second is unanswerable
until the first lands). Three sheets exist because the brief is wholly silent on three independent
physical rules that different downstream owners each need in a different form: whether the place
obeys ordinary physics (`05`), whether anything in it changes on its own (`06`), and how it is
traversed and what it keeps (`07`). Two sheets exist because the brief hands over settings facts
with no world rule attached: co-presence (`08`) and the aged materials it names without placing
them anywhere (`01`, `02`). The last (`09`) is a collection sheet, and it is the one the other five
leads and Art will read first: an explicit inventory of what this place contains and what it does
not, which is where the priority-3 exclusions become facts about the world instead of a process
rule everybody has to remember. **Dependency:** `03` before `04` and `07`; `01` and `02` before
`09`; `09` closes last but its negative half can be written from the brief alone on day one. Every
sheet ends in 2 to 4 criteria a build agent can check without judgment, per the category's
verification bar, and for a fiction sheet that usually means a term count, a stated absence, or an
observable state of the place rather than a number.

## Verification note

**`07 · passage-and-permanence` is the sheet most likely to be contradicted later**, and it is the
only one with three separate downstream owners already pulling in different directions before it is
written: arrangement and area order work *[currently: Gameplay — Meta & Content, wave 3]*, the
`areas` screen and any travel affordance *[UI/UX, wave 4]*, and the save-size decision that
`OPEN.md §2` already defaults to collapsing finished areas to a flag *[Tech & Data, wave 4]*. Any
world rule that keeps a restored area standing as a visitable place raises a persistence cost that
a later owner has a stated default to refuse; any rule that discards it contradicts priority 2's
plural *"visitable restored ruins"*. The sheet should expect a revision request from wave 3 or 4 and
should be written so that the contradiction, if it comes, is a one-line change rather than a rewrite.

**Second most likely: `05 · physical-law`**, contradicted by set-completion-bonus work in wave 3 if
it forbids a supernatural explanation those bonuses turn out to need, or by relic and effect work in
wave 4 if it permits one they do not want to render. **Third: `03 · scale-and-unit`**, which fixes
the noun-count every other category will use in naming, one to two waves before the arrangement
owner decides whether that count survives layout.

**Lowest risk: `09 · inventory`**, because its absent column is copied from binding decisions rather
than invented. Its failure mode is the opposite one: being too short to be useful.
