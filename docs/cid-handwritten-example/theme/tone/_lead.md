## What the brief gave me

- **A register, as three adjectives:** warm, aged, unhurried. `[brief: soft]`
- **A clarification:** "reclamation, not a haunted place" — warm and unhurried, not spooky.
  `[brief: soft]` `04-PRESENTATION.md`
- **Three prohibitions:** not spooky, not grim, not a power fantasy. `[brief: soft]`
- **One hard constraint:** zero tension is intended. `[brief: binding]` step 6 Q2. And its
  consequence, stated in the brief: *"audio and visual feedback carry the entire load, and
  nobody downstream should invent tension to fill the gap."*

## What the brief did not give me

**A humor level.** Nothing in 1,001 lines states one. This is the largest single ambiguity in
the category, because the genre convention for an 8–14 Roblox audience is loud and jokey, and
the stated register is the opposite. Two readers of "warm, aged, unhurried" will land in
different places on comedy, and naming, music, effects and store copy all read tone.

Decided in `02-humor-level.md` and tagged `[cid: decided]` so it stays visible. **This is a
question for the developer, not a decision I should be making** — flagged upward.

## Why four sheets

The register and the humor level are separate decisions that the brief's adjectives collapse
into one. The prohibitions need to be a list something can be checked against, not prose. And
with tension at zero, the emotional beat map is the load-bearing sheet in this domain: it is
the only thing telling Audio, VFX and Feedback UI where the game is allowed to peak.

| # | sheet | must decide |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | `register-baseline` | what "warm, aged, unhurried" means as a rule something can be checked against |
| 02 | `humor-level` | how funny this game is, and where humor is allowed to live |
| 03 | `tonal-prohibitions` | the checkable do-not list, including what falls out of zero tension |
| 04 | `emotional-beat-map` | where the game peaks, how often, and at what relative size |

## Verification note

`04-emotional-beat-map.md` is the sheet most likely to be contradicted later. Stingers Lead,
VFX Lead and Feedback UI Lead each independently want to add a moment. The beat map exists to
make that a conflict verification can see rather than a slow accumulation of noise.
